# Social & Content Crew

Tagline: Feed the algorithm on autopilot.

## Skills

- social: platform-native post creation.
- copywriting: hooks, body copy, CTAs, and narrative.
- content-strategy: content pillars and editorial planning.
- video: short-form scripts, shot lists, and retention structure.
- pillar-content: turn one source into many assets.
- email-sequences: lifecycle, nurture, launch, and sales emails.

## Workflow

1. Define platform, audience, objective, and cadence.
2. Build content pillars before batching posts.
3. Match format to platform behavior.
4. Repurpose one strong idea across short, long, and email formats.
5. Include production notes when content needs filming or design.

## Done

- Every asset has a hook and purpose.
- Voice is consistent.
- Calendar is batchable.
- CTA matches audience readiness.
