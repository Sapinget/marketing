# Operations Crew

Tagline: Run the business like a machine.

## Skills

- sop-builder: repeatable procedure documentation.
- incident-postmortem: timeline, root cause, and corrective action.
- business-case: decision memo with cost, benefit, risk, and alternatives.
- launch-runbook: launch plan, owners, checks, rollback.
- internal-comms: concise updates for teams and stakeholders.
- xlsx: spreadsheet structures, formulas, and operating trackers.

## Workflow

1. Define trigger, owner, input, output, and SLA.
2. Convert process into executable steps.
3. Add exception paths and escalation points.
4. Define metrics and review cadence.
5. Keep documents usable under pressure.

## Done

- Owner and handoffs are clear.
- Steps are actionable.
- Failure modes and rollback are included.
- Metrics reveal whether the process works.
