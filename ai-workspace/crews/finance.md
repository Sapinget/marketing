# Finance Crew

Tagline: Model the number before you spend.

## Skills

- dcf-model: discounted cash flow assumptions and outputs.
- 3-statements: income statement, balance sheet, cash flow model.
- lbo-model: leveraged buyout structure and returns.
- comps-analysis: comparable company and transaction analysis.
- pricing: pricing strategy, packaging, and unit economics.
- pitch-deck: investor narrative and financial story.

## Workflow

1. State assumptions before calculations.
2. Separate historicals, forecasts, and scenarios.
3. Identify key drivers and sensitivities.
4. Tie recommendations to cash, margin, payback, and risk.
5. Mark missing data clearly.

## Done

- Assumptions are explicit.
- Output is decision-ready.
- Scenarios show downside/base/upside.
- Financial advice limitations are stated when needed.
