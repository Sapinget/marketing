# Marketing Crew

Tagline: Copy, SEO and ads that convert.

## Skills

- seo-audit: technical and content SEO review.
- programmatic-seo: scalable page templates and keyword maps.
- ai-seo: AI search visibility and entity optimization.
- cro: conversion hypothesis, funnel friction, and experiment design.
- ad-creative: channel-native paid creative concepts.
- mktg-psychology: positioning, persuasion, and objection handling.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, offer, channel, and conversion action.
2. Separate claims from proof.
3. Map intent, objections, and funnel stage.
4. Produce variants that can be tested.
5. Include measurement and tracking requirements.

## Done

- Message is specific and testable.
- Copy has a clear hook, value, proof, and CTA.
- SEO recommendations map to search intent.
- Ads include angle, format, and success metric.
