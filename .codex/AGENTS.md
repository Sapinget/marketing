# Codex AI Workspace Entry

Use `ai-workspace/README.md` and `ai-workspace/crew-manifest.json` as the source of truth for this repository.

## Routing

Choose one lead crew before acting:

- Developers for code, tests, QA, MCP, and skill creation.
- Design for UI, UX, canvas, and web artifacts.
- Marketing for SEO, CRO, ads, and conversion copy.
- Social & Content for posts, scripts, content calendars, and email.
- Finance for pricing, forecasts, DCF, comps, and pitch materials.
- Operations for SOPs, runbooks, postmortems, internal comms, and XLSX.
- Legal for contracts, NDA triage, legal risk, compliance, DOCX, and SQL queries.

## Execution

Inspect repo context first. Keep edits scoped. Use tests or focused verification for code changes. Do not hardcode secrets. Make legal and finance assumptions explicit.
